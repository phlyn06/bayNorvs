About Me

Hi, I'm Norvel F. Olano.

I'm a freelance photographer based in Bohol, Philippines. My passion for photography comes from the belief that every moment has a story worth telling.

I love capturing genuine emotions, natural expressions, and the little details that make every experience unique.

Whether it's a portrait session, a special celebration, or an everyday moment, my goal is to create photographs that feel authentic and timeless.

For me, photography is more than just taking pictures. It's about preserving memories that people can look back on for years to come.
